\chapter{Conclusions}
\label{ch:conclusions}
\endgroup

The question this thesis was assigned was where to start, and the answer it arrived at
closes on itself.

We built a wavefunction and checked it against every external number that exists. The
checked wavefunction then became an instrument, and through it we watched a Wigner
molecule form: rings at definite occupancies, angular order stiffening as the trap
weakens, a shell radius growing as $\omega^{-2/3}$. Those are facts about electrons, and
they turned out to be precisely the facts a sampler needs in order to aim at the right part
of configuration space. Rebuilding the proposal out of the measured shell geometry carried
Markov-chain-free training from $\omega\approx0.01$ down to $\omega=0.0035$, into states
the original method could not reach. So the physics we learned from the solution improved
the solver, and the improved solver produced solutions we could not otherwise have learned
from. How far around that circle we got, and where it broke, is what remains to be set
down.

\section{What was asked, and what came back}

\paragraph{Architecture.} What does message passing buy that a separable network cannot?
A relational channel---and, measured through the tangent kernel, a specific one
(\S\ref{sec:kernel-q1}). A message-passing correlator reaches the same ground state along
half as many effective directions as a separable network of matched or larger capacity,
and those directions are the collective modes of the Hamiltonian rather than an arbitrary
basis. The separable network spends nearly half its tangent budget where no compact
physical dictionary finds it, matches the energy to four figures anyway, and carries a
higher local-energy variance for it at every confinement. In the nodes the same channel
shows up inverted: what message passing supplies at the Wigner crystal is not a
contribution but the absence of a collapse. A conventional backflow falls onto one mode
and costs half a percent; the copresheaf backflow, at that confinement, could be deleted
for $0.017\%$. Both channels give way at the same $\omega$, and a better correlator
postpones both.

\paragraph{Optimiser.} When does natural-gradient preconditioning earn its cost?
When the tangent metric is anisotropic \emph{and still estimable}
(\S\ref{sec:kernel-cond}). That is a narrower claim than the usual one and it has three
regimes rather than two. Under $|\Psi|^2$ sampling the metric is well enough conditioned
that Adam already suffices. On the collocation objective it is badly conditioned and
stochastic reconfiguration is what makes the optimisation work at all. And once the
sampling that builds the metric collapses, inverting it amplifies the noise rather than
the signal, and the method that was decisive becomes harmful. Ill-conditioning is not the
criterion. Estimability is.

\paragraph{Paradigm.} Can an accurate wavefunction be trained without a Markov chain? Yes,
inside a regime whose edges we had to find by walking off them
(\S\ref{sec:kernel-mcmcfree}). With no chain anywhere in the gradient, the weak-form
objective reaches DMC accuracy at $N\in\{6,12\}$ under strong confinement and stays
within a few tenths of a percent of our own converged energies well into the correlated
regime. It fails where the importance sampling loses the target, and it fails through one
chain of causes---proposal mismatch, weight-tail inflation, gradient noise, an
unestimable metric---which a better proposal moves rather than removes.

\paragraph{Physics.} Can the trained state serve as an instrument? It can, and the
evidence is that six independent diagnostics agree when they need not have
(\S\ref{sec:wigner-molecule}). Shell topologies match the classical Coulomb-cluster
predictions at every size we studied, with the competing sectors carrying the weight a
rotating molecule should give them. The reconstructions of $g(r)$ from shell geometry
alone reach cosine similarity $0.989$--$1.000$. At $N{=}20$ a three-shell molecule
collapses toward a single shell within one decade in $\omega$. None of this was put into
the ansatz, and none of it is visible in the density that one would naturally plot.

\section[The ledger]{What is established, what is consistent, and what is indicated}

The preface promised to be wrong on the record, which obliges a ledger. Three kinds of
claim run through this thesis, they are not interchangeable, and the difference between
them is easy to lose in a summary.

\emph{Established against something outside this thesis.} The energies for $N\le12$ at
$\omega\ge0.1$, and for $N{=}2$ down to $\omega=0.01$. Two independent methods agree
there to a few hundredths of a percent. That is the whole of the external ground truth,
and every relative error quoted anywhere else in this work depends on a reference we
computed ourselves.

\emph{Internally consistent, and consistent in ways that could have failed.} The
deep-Wigner energies, the structural picture, and the architecture comparison. Nothing
here is validated from outside, but the internal agreements are not cheap: independent
diagnostics that measure different things converge on the same crossover; the fitted
energy exponent lands near the classical $2/3$ that nothing in the ansatz knows about;
the shell topologies reproduce classical ground states nobody trained the network to
reproduce; and the architecture result survives seeding, a change of probe measure, and
a capacity ladder that runs against it.

\emph{Indicated, and no more.} How the tangent-space picture scales with size. The
architecture comparison is seeded at $N{=}6$, rests on fewer models at $N{=}12$, and on a
single production state at $N{=}20$. The trend runs in the direction the mechanism predicts
and widens with particle number, but it is not settled, and settling it is a question of
compute rather than of interpretation.

One further entry belongs here, methodological rather than physical. Quantities defined on
a single component of the ansatz---the backflow's rank, its displacement magnitude, its
share of the energy---are gauge-like, because the Jastrow and the backflow are substitutes
and the division of labour between them is a nearly flat direction of the loss that the
training route settles rather than the state. Every claim that reversed during this project
was of that kind. The conclusions above rest only on what can be measured on the state
itself or by deleting part of it.

\section{Where it stops, and what would move it}

The Markov-chain-free route is bounded by two walls of different kinds. The first is
statistical: the proposal loses the target, and a better proposal moves the wall, in our
case from $\omega\approx0.01$ to $\omega=0.0035$ once the proposal was built out of the
physics. The second does not yield to sampling at all. Below $\omega=0.0035$ a converged
state fails to survive transfer to a weaker trap, because the Wigner peak exists only
through a near-cancellation between an exponentially suppressed determinant and a Jastrow
that amplifies it back, and a $23\%$ change in $\omega$ tips that balance. Walkers placed
directly on the classical shell relax inward regardless, so no proposal can repair it.
Reaching further would take an $\ell$-covariant parametrisation, or an objective anchored
on the shell amplitude rather than on the energy alone.

Three other things would move the picture, in rough order of how much. A backflow that is
not quadratic in particle number: the $\mathcal{O}(N^2 h)$ edge tensor
is what stops the method at twenty electrons, and it stops it for reasons of memory
rather than of physics. A proposal that adapts on its own---a normalising flow, or the
shell-informed construction of \S\ref{sec:kernel-q3-frontier} generalised past ring
geometries. And the same architecture study repeated at $N{=}12$ and $N{=}20$ with the
seeding the $N{=}6$ comparison already has, which would turn the last entry of the ledger
from indicated into established.

\section{A closing thought}

What the network is, how it is optimised, and what measure defines its loss looked at the
outset like three separate engineering choices, and the finding that recurs through every
chapter is that they are one question about one matrix. Whether that is a fact about neural
networks seems doubtful. It looks more like a fact about the problem: capacity was never
the scarce resource, and what mattered instead was staying aligned with the
low-dimensional collective structure the physics already has---which, in real space, is the
Wigner molecule.

The durable results came from the places where the attempt failed. A rank collapse that
survived seeding and a dimension inversion that did not. A sampler blamed for a wall the
ansatz had built. An importance-sampling bug whose signature, accuracy falling away at
larger $N$ and weaker $\omega$, was exactly what an inadequate architecture would have
produced, and which no amount of thinking about architectures would ever have found. Each
of those cost months, and each of them is in this thesis rather than quietly absent from
it, because a mistake that has been written down can be argued with.
