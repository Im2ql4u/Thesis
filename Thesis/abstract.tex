\chapter*{Abstract}
This thesis studies how far a compact, well-conditioned neural network can be taken as a
variational wavefunction for interacting electrons, what a relational architecture
contributes that a separable one does not, and whether such a state can be trained without
a Markov chain. The system is the two-dimensional harmonic quantum dot, from the compact,
kinetic-energy-dominated droplet at strong confinement to the dilute Wigner molecule at
weak confinement, for \(N\in\{2,6,12,20\}\) electrons and trap frequencies \(\omega\) from
\(1\) down to \(10^{-3}\).

The ansatz is a Slater determinant dressed by a neural Jastrow correlator, which reshapes
the amplitude, and a coordinate backflow, which reshapes the nodes. Both are constructed to
be safe for Laplacian-based training: coordinates are scaled to trap units, pair features
are soft-core, and the electron--electron cusp is imposed analytically. Each learnable
object is built in two forms---one that pools per-particle and pairwise features in a
single pass, and one that passes messages between particles on a graph---and the trained
states are read through the geometry of their log-derivatives, the quantum geometric tensor
and the neural tangent kernel. Training is by residual pretraining followed by stochastic
reconfiguration (SR), and, separately, by importance-sampled collocation that uses no
Markov chain.

Energies match diffusion Monte Carlo within uncertainty for \(N=2\); for
\(N\in\{6,12\}\) the message-passing ansatz stays within \(0.0008\!-\!0.04\%\) and the
separable one within \(0.008\!-\!0.05\%\) wherever a DMC reference exists
(\(\omega\ge0.1\)). At \(N=20\), beyond the published DMC grid, the energies lie within
\(0.04\%\) of the tabulated variational energies of an earlier master's thesis. Where a
reference exists it also serves as the pretraining target, so what these numbers report is
an independently evaluated variational upper bound agreeing with the reference, not two
blind calculations meeting. Below \(\omega=0.1\) no external benchmark exists for
\(N\ge6\), and that boundary is marked wherever it occurs.

Message passing introduces a relational channel, and its effect is visible in the tangent
geometry before it is visible in the energy. At strong confinement the message-passing
correlator reaches reference-quality solutions along roughly half as many effective tangent
directions as a single-pass network of matched or larger capacity needs; at every
confinement it keeps its leading tangent directions inside a compact dictionary of the
Hamiltonian's collective modes where the separable network does not; and its local-energy
variance is lower throughout, by factors between \(1.2\) and \(3.4\)---while the two
networks agree in energy to four figures. In the nodes the same
channel shows as a collapse averted: toward the Wigner crystal a conventional backflow
falls onto a single collective displacement mode and loses half a percent of the energy,
where the message-passing field retains its rank. Correlator and backflow give way at the
same confinement, which supports a common relational bottleneck rather than two independent
deficits. Stochastic reconfiguration earns its cost only where the tangent metric is
both anisotropic and still estimable: under \(|\Psi|^2\) sampling, where the empirical
metric is badly conditioned but well estimated, it changes nothing that Adam does not
already do (tested at \(N\le6\)); under collocation it is what makes the optimisation work
at all; and once the sampling that builds the metric collapses, inverting it does harm. Importance-sampled collocation matches SR--VMC while its sampling stays
healthy and reaches a Wigner molecule at \(\omega=0.0035\) with no Markov chain in the
optimisation. Two limits bound that route: a statistical one, which a proposal built from the measured
shell geometry moves back by a factor of three in \(\omega\), and beyond it a failure of
transfer between confinements, arising from a fine-tuned Slater--Jastrow cancellation that
no change of proposal repairs.

The trained states are then used to map the Fermi-liquid-to-Wigner crossover from the
inside. We give topology-resolved shell, bond-orientational, and stiffness diagnostics,
with a polygon shell model fitted on held-out frames reproducing the measured pair
distribution to cosine similarity \(0.987\!-\!0.999\), against \(0.936\!-\!0.996\) for an
angle-randomised null: at \(\omega=10^{-3}\) the \(N=6\) dot is a single
ring with a robust \((1,5)\leftrightarrow(0,6)\) near-degeneracy (radial mode
\(\approx114\,a_0\)), the \(N=12\) dot a two-shell molecule with a modal \((3,9)\) split,
and the \(N=20\) dot a three-shell molecule that collapses to a single ring about a central
electron within one decade in \(\omega\). The measured \(N=6\) shell radius approaches the
classical Wigner value \(1.334\,\omega^{-2/3}\) monotonically, to within \(1.7\%\) at the
weakest trap, and it and the angular order are the inputs to the physics-informed proposal
described above.

Two limits on scope apply throughout. All states reported here are the lowest found within
a fixed spin-projection sector; total spin is not scanned. And the architecture comparison
is seeded at \(N=6\), rests on fewer trained models at \(N=12\), and on a single production
state at \(N=20\).
