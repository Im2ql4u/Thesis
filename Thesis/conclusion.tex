\chapter{Conclusions}
\label{ch:conclusions}
\endgroup

The work formed a feedback cycle between solver and physics. A variational state was
constructed and checked against every external benchmark available; the checked state was
then used as an instrument, and resolved the shell geometry of the Wigner regime---rings at
definite occupancies, $n$-fold angular order becoming large only in the last decade of
confinement, a shell radius approaching its classical $\omega^{-2/3}$ scaling. Those measurements are what a sampler needs in order to place its mass
correctly. Incorporating the measured geometry into the proposal extended
Markov-chain-free training from the failure of the origin-centred sampler at
$\omega\approx0.01$ down to $\omega=0.0035$, into states the original method could not
reach.

\section{The research questions answered}

\paragraph{Architecture.} What does message passing buy that a separable network cannot?
A relational channel---and, measured through the tangent kernel, a specific one
(\S\ref{sec:kernel-q1}). At strong confinement a message-passing correlator reaches a reference-quality solution
along half as many effective directions as a separable network of matched or larger
capacity---the two agree in energy to four figures, which is not by itself evidence that
they are the same state---and its leading directions are far more completely accounted for
by a compact dictionary of the Hamiltonian's collective modes than the separable network's
are. The separable network spends nearly half its tangent budget where no compact
physical dictionary finds it, matches the energy to four figures anyway, and carries a
higher local-energy variance for it at every confinement. In the nodes the same channel
shows up inverted: what message passing supplies at the Wigner crystal is not a
contribution but the absence of a collapse. A conventional backflow falls onto one mode
and costs half a percent; the copresheaf backflow, at that confinement, can be deleted
for $0.017\%$. Both channels give way at the same $\omega$, and a better correlator
postpones both.

\paragraph{Optimiser.} When does natural-gradient preconditioning earn its cost?
When the tangent metric is anisotropic \emph{and still estimable}
(\S\ref{sec:kernel-cond}). That is a narrower claim than the usual one and it has three
regimes rather than two. Under $|\Psi|^2$ sampling the empirical metric is
badly conditioned---$\kappa(S)\sim10^{7}$--$10^{10}$ on the very models where Adam and SR
agree to four decimals---so conditioning alone cannot be what makes SR unnecessary there.
What distinguishes the regimes, on the evidence we have, is whether a batch can resolve the
metric. Under $|\Psi|^2$ sampling the samples come from the measure
that defines $S$, and Adam already moves along the directions carrying Fisher weight. On the collocation objective they do not, and under a fixed proposal
stochastic reconfiguration is what makes the optimisation work at all. That is a statement
about the bare objective: once adaptive proposal refitting, oversampling and rollback are in
place they do the same conditioning work, and Adam---which degrades more gracefully when the
sampling fails---becomes the production choice. And once the
sampling that builds the metric collapses, inverting it amplifies the noise instead of the
signal, and the method that was decisive becomes harmful. The negative half of that is
measured: a large $\kappa(S)$ does not imply SR will help. The positive half---that the
collocation objective under a fixed proposal occupies an anisotropic but still estimable
middle regime---is the mechanism the evidence points to rather than one we measured
directly, since $\kappa$ was never obtained under the collocation measure at matched batch
size. Estimability, not conditioning alone, is what the data identify as decisive.

\paragraph{Paradigm.} Can an accurate wavefunction be trained without a Markov chain? Yes,
inside a regime whose edges we had to find by walking off them
(\S\ref{sec:kernel-mcmcfree}). With no chain anywhere in the gradient, the weak-form
objective reaches DMC accuracy at $N\in\{6,12\}$ under strong confinement and stays
within a few tenths of a percent of our own converged energies well into the correlated
regime. It fails where the importance sampling loses the target, and it fails through one
chain of causes---proposal mismatch, weight-tail inflation, gradient noise, an
unestimable metric---which a better proposal moves but does not remove.

\paragraph{Physics.} Can the trained state serve as an instrument? It can, and the
evidence is that six diagnostics measuring different things agree when they need not have
(\S\ref{sec:wigner-molecule}). At $\omega=10^{-3}$ the identified topology at each particle number matches the
corresponding classical Coulomb-cluster pattern, with the competing sectors carrying the
weight a rotating molecule should give them; at stronger confinement the occupancy labels
describe a radial partition rather than a crystalline topology. A polygon shell model fitted on half the frames
reproduces $g(r)$ on the other half to cosine similarity $0.987$--$0.999$, against
$0.936$--$0.996$ for an angle-randomised null. The measured $N{=}6$ ring radius approaches
the classical Wigner value to within $1.7\%$ at the weakest trap. And at $N{=}20$ a
three-shell molecule collapses to a single ring about a central electron within one decade
in $\omega$. None of this was put into the ansatz, and none of the angular or
topology-resolved ordering is visible in the lab-frame density one would naturally
plot---though the radial shelling is.

\section{Evidential status of the findings}

The findings fall into three evidential categories, which are not interchangeable.

\emph{Externally benchmarked.} The energies for $N\in\{6,12,20\}$ at $\omega\ge0.1$, and for
$N{=}2$ down to $\omega=0.01$---the twenty-electron dot included, since fixed-node DMC exists
there too (Table~\ref{tab:energies}). An independently evaluated variational upper bound agrees
with the external reference there to a few hundredths of a percent. The two are not blind
of one another---the reference is also the pretraining target where it exists
(\S\ref{sec:energy-discussion})---but a variational energy is bounded below by $E_0$
whatever it was aimed at, so agreement from above cannot be manufactured by aiming. That is
the whole of the external benchmark coverage, and, as \S\ref{subsec:dmc-theory} makes
clear, a fixed-node DMC value is itself an approximation, not ground truth. Every
relative error quoted outside that range depends on a reference we computed ourselves.

\emph{Internally supported.} The deep-Wigner energies, the structural picture, and the
architecture comparison. None is validated from outside, but each rests on agreements that could have failed and did
not. They are not equally demanding, and are worth ranking. The strongest are the ones with
a discriminating null or a discrete prediction: the shell topologies reproduce the classical
ground-state configurations at every size, and the held-out polygon model beats an
angle-randomised null by a margin that grows monotonically as the trap weakens. Next comes
the architecture result, which survives seeding, a change of probe measure, and a capacity
ladder that runs against it. The scaling checks---the fitted energy exponent near the
classical $2/3$, the ring radius within $1.7\%$ of the classical prefactor---are genuine, and
neither number enters the ansatz anywhere, but they are also the checks an ansatz is most
likely to pass, since the physics is becoming classical in precisely the limit where they
are evaluated. What the crossover region itself still lacks is a comparably at-risk
prediction.

\emph{Indicated but not established.} How the tangent-space picture scales with size. The
architecture comparison is seeded at $N{=}6$, rests on fewer models at $N{=}12$, and on a
single production state at $N{=}20$. The trend runs in the direction the mechanism predicts
and widens with particle number, but it is not settled, and settling it is a question of
compute, not of interpretation.

One further entry belongs here, methodological instead of physical. The conclusions above rest on
quantities measured on the state itself, supported by ablations that are reproducible for a
fixed protocol without being state invariants, and not on the component-level ranks and
magnitudes whose gauge dependence \S\ref{sec:synthesis} sets out.

\section{Limitations and further work}

The Markov-chain-free route is bounded by two walls of different kinds
(\S\ref{sec:collocation-discussion}). The first is statistical, and a better proposal moved
it from $\omega\approx0.01$ to $\omega=0.0035$. The second is a failure of transfer: below $\omega=0.0035$ the ansatz is not covariant
enough in $\ell$ for a converged state to survive being carried to a weaker trap, and no
proposal repairs that. Whether the ansatz could represent such a state under per-$\omega$
re-optimisation is a separate question we did not test. Reaching further would take
an $\ell$-covariant parametrisation, or an objective anchored on the shell amplitude rather
than on the energy alone.

Three other things would move the picture, in rough order of how much. A backflow that is
not quadratic in particle number, since it is the $\mathcal{O}(N^2 h)$ edge tensor and not
the physics that stops the method at twenty electrons. A proposal that adapts on its own---a normalising flow, or the
shell-informed construction of \S\ref{sec:kernel-q3-frontier} generalised past ring
geometries. And the same architecture study repeated at $N{=}12$ and $N{=}20$ with the
seeding the $N{=}6$ comparison already has, which would turn the last entry of the ledger
from indicated into established.

\section{Concluding perspective}

At the production widths reached for the well-resolved $N\le12$ studies, raw representational
capacity was rarely the first bottleneck; alignment, conditioning and sampling were. (It was
not irrelevant everywhere: at $N{=}20$ the memory cost of the backflow's edge tensor forces a
capacity cut that degrades the collocation gradients.) What determined the outcome, across
architecture, optimisation and sampling alike, was alignment with the low-dimensional
collective structure the Hamiltonian already possesses---breathing, correlation-hole and
relative modes at strong confinement, resolving at weak confinement into the shell geometry
of the Wigner molecule. That the same alignment matters in all three places is probably a
fact about the system rather than about neural networks.

Several of the results reported here were reached only after an initial reading of the same
data proved wrong: a rank collapse that survived seeding where a dimension inversion did
not, a transfer failure first attributed to the sampler and later to the ansatz, and an
importance-sampling error whose signature was indistinguishable from an architectural
deficiency. Each was identified by a diagnostic that could separate the competing
explanations, which is the methodological point the preceding chapters have tried to make
concrete: a variational calculation is only as trustworthy as the tests that can tell its
failure modes apart.
