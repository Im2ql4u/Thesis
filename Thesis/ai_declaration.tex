\chapter*{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}

Artificial-intelligence systems were used as discussion partners during the
development of this thesis. The use was limited to:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.25em}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
	\item discussing results and possible failure modes;
	\item planning experiments and next steps; and
	\item reviewing the prose for clarity and mistakes.
\end{itemize}
The author wrote the code and thesis text, and made the scientific decisions.
