\chapter*{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}

Artificial-intelligence systems were used as discussion partners during the
development of this thesis.  They were useful for interrogating results,
identifying possible failure modes, planning experiments, and reviewing prose
for clarity and mistakes.  The scientific decisions, the implementation, and
the text of this thesis were written by the author.  Agents were used to review
the writing, not to author it.

The exchange below captures one moment in the development of the collocation
approach.  It is included because it illustrates a real methodological tension:
after months of unsuccessful work, an apparently definitive recommendation to
abandon the approach had to be treated as a hypothesis to test, rather than as
a conclusion to accept.  Substantial work occurred between the responses shown;
the second and third replies are presented in their original, non-chronological
order.
