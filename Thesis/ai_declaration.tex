\color{mutedblue}\textbf{Use of AI in this thesis}\par\smallskip
\color{black}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.25em}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
	\item AI systems were used as discussion partners to interrogate results,
	identify possible failure modes, and plan experiments.
	\item Agents reviewed the prose for clarity and mistakes; they did not author
	the text.
	\item The author wrote the code and thesis text, and made the scientific
	decisions.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}}
